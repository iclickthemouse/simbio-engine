How it works

Add the skill to Claude

Turn on code execution in Claude's settings, then go to Customize, Skills and upload simbio-onboarding.zip (leave it zipped).

Tell Claude about your company

Share your website, a few documents or a leader interview. Claude drafts a one-page fact sheet for you to check.

Share the link

Claude builds the game, plays it end to end to check it, and hands you a private link or an LMS package.

Start a new chat and say: "Make a SimBio onboarding for our company. Here's our website: …"

Questions

What do I need?

A Claude plan with code execution turned on. Nothing to install on your computer, and no coding. If the menus look different from the steps above, search Anthropic's help center for "Use skills in Claude".

Is it really free?

Yes. SimBio and the Claude skill are free to use through the end of 2026, and any edition you start before 2027 stays free for good, updates included. From 2027, building new editions may be paid. The source is on GitHub so you can see exactly how it works. Details are in the SimBio Free License.

What about the illustrations?

SimBio uses 20 free illustrations from Storyset. Their license doesn't allow bundling them, so the first time you build, Claude gives you the 20 links. Download them once (about 10 minutes) and Claude prepares them for you. You can also skip them.

Is it accurate?

Every company fact needs a source, nothing is invented, and investigational medicines are described the way your company describes them publicly. Still, have communications, legal or compliance read the fact sheet before it goes to every employee.
